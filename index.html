How Gritto works

Drills — pick a sport, then describe what you're working on or upload a short video. Gritto gives you real drills with a cue and an animated demo for each.

Daily Routine — build a short 5–10 minute routine around one goal. Check it off each day to build a streak, and log how long you practiced.

Home — your greeting, both streaks, recent activity, and your video history all in one place.

Cookies & Local Storage

Here's exactly what Gritto uses, and why.

Essential — Sign-in & Preferences Always on

Local storage on your device that keeps you signed in and remembers basic settings. Gritto can't work without this — there's no way to turn it off and still use the app.

Google Sign-In Set by Google

When you use "Sign in with Google," Google's own sign-in service may set cookies on their domain as part of that process — separate from anything Gritto sets. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not Gritto's.

Advertising & tracking None

Gritto doesn't use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies of any kind.
